# Pitch — Holdout (working title)

## One paragraph
The world ended in rust. Holdout is a co-op (1–5) open-world wave survival game in a post-apocalyptic scrapland. By day your squad roams the wasteland: chopping dead trees, quarrying stone, stripping scrap, looting wrecks and bunkers, crafting gear, and fortifying a base. By night the mutants come, hunting by sound, and the walls, turrets and traps you built fight alongside you. Survive 12 nights and kill the night-12 boss to win; every run earns meta-currency for permanent unlocks back in the Lobby.

## The twist
- **Noise.** Everything loud fills a shared squad Noise meter: chopping, quarrying, breaking loot crates, crafting at a bench, building, gunfire. At dusk the meter sets that night's wave size and mix, and it also multiplies the loot that drops at dawn. Every day becomes a squad decision: play quiet and safe, or loud and greedy. Quiet tools, silencers and muffled workbenches become craftable goals. The meter resets each dawn (some upgrades lower the floor).
- **Salvage war.** Mutants drop parts (gears, plating, cores). These parts are the only way to upgrade turrets and traps. Night feeds day: loud nights are dangerous, but they drop more parts.

## Core loop
Lobby: pick a loadout, spend meta-currency → **Day**: explore, gather, loot points of interest, craft, build → **Dusk**: Noise is locked in → **Night**: defend the base against waves; turrets and traps fight alongside you → **Dawn**: rewards (Noise-scaled), repair → repeat → **Run end** (night 12 boss, or squad wiped): earn meta-currency → Lobby.

## Run structure
- 12 nights; boss on night 12. Target run length ~30–45 minutes (tune day/night lengths).
- Squad wiped = run lost (partial meta-currency by nights survived).
- Meta-progression only: currency, unlocks, cosmetics persist; each run starts fresh.

## Resources
Wood, stone, scrap (gathered) · salvage parts (from enemies) · loot (weapons, gear, blueprints) from points of interest.

## Inspirations (feel only, nothing copied)
- Open-world survival modes: gather/build by day, survive by night.
- Roblox co-op night survival (e.g. 99 Nights in the Forest): the "explore further vs go back and prepare" tension. Its known weakness is repetition; Noise + varied POIs answer that.
- Roblox wave TD (e.g. Tower Defense Simulator): loadout before the match, escalating waves, meaningful defenses.

## Audience & platforms
Roblox players 10+; PC main, console and mobile from day one. Short sessions must feel complete (one night is a satisfying chunk).
